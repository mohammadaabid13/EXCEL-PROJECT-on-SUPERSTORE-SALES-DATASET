Key Strategic Recommendations
Executive Roadmap
1. Restructure South Region

2. Rationalize Sub-Categories

Renegotiate vendor contracts or adjust retail pricing on systemic loss-making product lines—primarily Tables (-$7.2K loss), Rubber Bands, and Scissors—to halt gross margin bleed enterprise-wide.

3. Scale High-Margin Assets

Expand promotional backing for Office Machines ($318.1K total revenue) and Binders ($59.3K profit), deploying Central's 17.26% margin efficiency model across East and West operations.
